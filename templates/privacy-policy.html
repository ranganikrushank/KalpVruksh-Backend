🔒 Payment Processing & Razorpay

PCI-DSS Level 1 Compliant

All payment transactions are securely processed through Razorpay, India's leading payment gateway. We never handle or store your full payment credentials.

What Razorpay Handles

  • ✓ Card number, CVV & expiry encryption
  • ✓ 3D Secure authentication flows
  • ✓ Tokenization for recurring payments
  • ✓ Fraud detection & risk scoring

What We Store

  • • Transaction ID & reference number
  • • Payment status & amount
  • • Last 4 digits of card (for display)
  • • Payment method type (e.g., "Visa")

For complete details, review Razorpay's Privacy Policy and Security Practices.

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

Trusted Service Providers

Vendors who help us operate (hosting, analytics, email delivery) under strict data processing agreements. Examples: AWS, Razorpay, SendGrid.

Legal Requirements

When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect rights, safety, and property.

Business Transfers

In connection with merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. Users will be notified via email and/or prominent notice before data transfer.

Subprocessors: A current list of our subprocessors and their roles is available at atlanciaworldwide.com/subprocessors.

Retention Periods

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Account data: Duration of active subscription + 30 days post-cancellation
  • Transaction records: 7 years (to comply with Indian tax laws)
  • Support communications: 2 years from resolution
  • Analytics data: Aggregated/anonymized indefinitely; raw data deleted after 13 months

Security Measures

Technical Safeguards

AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, regular penetration testing, SOC 2 Type II compliance

Organizational Controls

Role-based access, mandatory security training, incident response plan, quarterly audits
